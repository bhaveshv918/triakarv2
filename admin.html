Business & tax settings
These settings decide intra-state (CGST + SGST) vs inter-state (IGST) tax and the default HSN. They are saved in this browser. A product's own hsn_code / gst_rate overrides the defaults.
What's in the workbook
Summary — period totals, tax split, and order counts
Sales Register — every invoice line: customer, address, place of supply, product, HSN, qty, taxable value, IGST / CGST / SGST
Invoices — one row per invoice with totals, status, and payment reference
b2cl — inter-state B2C invoices above ₹1,00,000 (GSTR-1 Table 5)
b2cs — other B2C sales by place of supply and rate (GSTR-1 Table 7)
exp — international orders (GSTR-1 Table 6A)
hsn — HSN-wise summary (GSTR-1 Table 12)
docs — invoice series issued and cancelled (GSTR-1 Table 13)
